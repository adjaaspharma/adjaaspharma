# ADJAAS Pharmaceuticals
Responsive, build-free company website. Open dist/index.html or run `python3 -m http.server 8080 --directory dist`.

## Files
- dist/index.html: company content and accessible page structure
- dist/style.css: responsive navy/teal design
- dist/app.js: product enquiries, mission accordion, mobile menu, email enquiry
- dist/assets/adjaas-logo.jpeg: supplied company logo

Contact enquiries open the visitor's email app; no backend submission or personal-data database is configured. Product categories are descriptive only; actual formulations, regulatory credentials and product availability must be supplied by the company before adding them. Supplied company copy is used without inventing certifications, product approvals, customer statistics or testimonials.

Design references: feragyl.com (company, product and contact structure). lifecompharma.com was reviewed in the browser for its science-led narrative, therapeutic category navigation and partnership structure. No reference site assets or text were copied. User video was not available in the chat at build time.

Hero, research, product-range, sustainability and healthcare-partnership photos are AI-generated illustrations. They do not depict the company’s actual premises, staff or products. Replace with verified company photography when available.

## Deployment
The site is static and has no installation or build step. Publish the `dist` directory. A `vercel.json` file configures this output folder for Vercel.

## Features
- Responsive company website with navy and medical teal branding
- Animated transparent hero cutout with a pause control
- Seven product categories, each with its own AI-generated photo
- Company, values, vision, mission and partnership sections
- Mobile navigation, product dropdown and mission accordions
- Click-to-call and email enquiries
